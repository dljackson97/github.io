About Clementine Speech Therapy

A warm, collaborative speech‑language practice supporting communication across the lifespan in St. Petersburg, FL.

Meet your SLP

Aemelia Jackson, M.S., CCC‑SLP

I'm Aemelia Jackson, a licensed Speech‑Language Pathologist and the owner of Clementine Speech Therapy. I work with children, teens, and adults to build communication skills that feel natural, functional, and empowering.

My approach is warm, collaborative, and grounded in evidence‑based practice. I value connection and believe that therapy should fit into real life — whether that's playing on the floor with a child, supporting a teen in the classroom, or helping an adult feel more confident at work.

Philosophy

Communication as connection

I believe everyone deserves to feel understood. Therapy at Clementine is built on:

  • Functional, meaningful goals.
  • Collaboration with families, caregivers, and professionals.
  • Clear, compassionate communication.
  • Celebrating progress at every stage of life.
