What we do

More than milestones:
communication that blooms in daily life

At Clementine Speech Therapy, we help people of all ages build the communication and feeding skills that support connection, independence, and daily life. Our services span early communication, speech clarity, language, social communication, fluency, cognitive‑communication, and feeding and swallowing.

We know that every child is unique, and it is our mission to identify strengths, address communication challenges, and work alongside parents and teachers to attain meaningful goals. Our clinical expertise includes supporting speech delays and disorders, childhood apraxia of speech, language delays and disorders, gestalt language processing, late talkers, stuttering, executive functioning, reading challenges, dyslexia, and spelling and writing needs.

For adults, we take the same individualized, holistic approach, supporting communication after stroke, brain injury, or neurological changes with care that honors each person’s lived experience.

With warm guidance and evidence‑based practice, we partner with families and adults throughout their journey, creating therapy that feels supportive, functional, and truly meaningful.
