How It Works

A clear, collaborative process designed to support communication at every stage of life.

Our process

From first conversation to ongoing support

Step 1

Consultation

A brief conversation to learn about your goals, answer questions, and determine whether services are a good fit.

Step 2

Screening or evaluation

A developmentally appropriate, evidence‑based assessment tailored to age, needs, and concerns.

Step 3

Personalized plan

Clear goals and a collaborative plan focused on functional communication in daily life.

Step 4

Therapy sessions

Engaging, individualized sessions offered at home, in community settings, or online, depending on your needs.

Step 5

Ongoing support

Regular communication, progress updates, and practical strategies to carry skills into everyday routines.

Collaboration

Working as a team

With your permission, we can collaborate with teachers, caregivers, and other professionals to support consistent progress.
