What we offer

Services tailored to every voice

Evidence‑based speech‑language services for children, teens, and adults designed to support clear, confident communication at every stage of life.

Core services

Empowering all communication, every age

From foundational first words to adult rehabilitation, services are designed to meet each person where they are and help them grow.

Speech sound & articulation

Clear, confident speech

Support for children, teens, and adults who want clearer, more precise speech and the confidence to use it every day.

  • Articulation
  • Phonological processes
  • Motor planning & execution (including Childhood Apraxia of Speech)
  • Speech clarity & intelligibility
Language

Understanding & expression

Support for receptive and expressive language across all ages, from early communication to complex academic and social language.

  • Early communication & late talkers
  • Expressive & receptive language
  • Listening & processing
  • Vocabulary, grammar, and sentence structure
  • Reading, writing, spelling
  • Gestalt language processing
  • Pragmatics (social communication)
  • Prelinguistic communication (joint attention, intentionality, communicative signaling)
  • Paralinguistic communication (gestures, signs, body language)
Cognitive Communication

Thinking skills for daily life

Support for cognitive skills that impact communication, learning, and independence; especially after stroke, brain injury, or neurological changes.

  • Attention
  • Memory
  • Problem solving
  • Executive functioning
Fluency

Smooth, confident communication

Support for individuals experiencing disruptions in speech flow.

  • Stuttering
  • Cluttering
Voice & Resonance

Healthy, functional voice

Support for indivudals experiencing changes in voice quality, pitch, loudness, or resonance.

  • Phonation quality
  • Pitch & loudness
  • Alaryngeal voice
  • Hypernasality
  • Hyponasality
  • Cul-de-sac resonance
  • Forward focus
Adult communication

Work & daily life

For adults seeking clearer speech, stronger strategies, and more confident communication in every setting.

  • Articulation and clarity
  • Expressive language and organization
  • Communication confidence in daily interactions
Feeding & Swallowing

Safe, confident mealtimes

Support for infants, children, and adults experiencing feeding or swallowing challenges.

  • Oral phase
  • Pharyngeal phase
  • Esophageal phase
  • Atypical eating (food selectivity/refusal, negative physiologic response)
  • Pediatric feeding support
  • Adult dysphagia related to stroke, TBI, or neurological changes
Auditory Habilitation & Rehabilitation

Listening & communication skills

Support for individuals with hearing loss, deafness, or auditory processing challenges.

  • Speech, language, and listening skills impacted by hearing loss
  • Auditory processing

Additional support

Beyond the session: whole‑life support

Progress happens in the real world. These services extend communication support well beyond the therapy room.

Caregiver & family coaching

Support beyond the session

Practical strategies and coaching so communication growth continues at home, in school, and in the community. You are your child's most important communication partner.

Community & center partnerships

consultations & collaboration

consultations, on‑site services, and collaboration with early‑learning programs, schools, and community organizations to reach more people with quality care.
