About Aemelia

Therapy built on connection and trust

I'm Aemelia Jackson, a licensed Speech‑Language Pathologist and the owner of Clementine Speech Therapy. I work with children, teens, and adults to build communication skills that feel natural, functional, and empowering.

My approach is warm, collaborative, and grounded in evidence‑based practice. I believe therapy should fit into real life; whether that's playing on the floor with a child, supporting a teen in the classroom, or helping an adult rebuild communication after a stroke or brain injury. Sessions are shaped around each person's strengths, needs, and goals, with space for flexibility, connection, and meaningful progress.

Communication is at the heart of every relationship, every opportunity, and every moment of self‑expression. I'm honored to walk alongside my clients and families as they build skills that fosters connection and supports confidence in daily life.
